Exact scores are paying out twice as often this month

Across the last five rounds the exact-score market landed on 18% of cards — nearly double the season average. Most of that comes from low-scoring league games, where 1–0 and 2–1 keep repeating. The outcome market is still the safer 10 points, but if you are chasing the table, the 30 is where the ground is being made up right now.

Private leagues are outgrowing the public boards

Nine in ten rounds are now played inside an invite-only league rather than on the global table. Smaller boards mean your position moves every single round, and the chat keeps everyone honest about their picks. Public leagues still decide the season ranking, but the day-to-day competition has clearly moved indoors.

What the derby round means for your streak

Six top-flight derbies land in the same round for the first time this season. Derbies break form: home advantage counts for less, and draws come in well above the usual rate. If your streak is on the line, the outcome market is the one to lean on this week — the exact score can wait for a calmer round.
